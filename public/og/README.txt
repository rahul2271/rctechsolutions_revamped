OG IMAGES — CREATE THESE BEFORE DEPLOYING
==========================================

These 3 images are referenced in your site metadata but don't exist yet.
Without them, social media shares show a blank preview (no image).

Required files (1200 × 630 px, JPEG or WebP, under 200KB each):

  home-cover.jpg   — Homepage social share image
                     Suggested: RC Tech Solutions logo + "Web Development
                     Company in Mohali, Punjab" on a dark/brand background

  blogs-cover.jpg  — Blog listing social share image
                     Suggested: "The Journal" heading with RC Tech branding

  about-cover.jpg  — About page social share image
                     Suggested: Rahul Chauhan photo + name + title

Tools (free):
  - Canva: canva.com → custom size 1200×630
  - Figma: figma.com → frame 1200×630
  - Adobe Express: express.adobe.com

After creating, place them in this folder (/public/og/) and redeploy.
